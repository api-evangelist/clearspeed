# Clearspeed

> Clearspeed analyzes voice signals in real time to assess risk, accelerate vetting, prevent fraud, and enable trusted decisions at scale. Its voice-based risk assessment technology flags elevated risk at the point of a claim, application, or interaction while reducing friction for the low-risk majority.

Clearspeed serves insurance, government, defense, and financial services organizations. The site is organized into solution/industry pages, use cases, client stories, and a resource hub (blog, podcasts, videos, webinars, whitepapers). Collection index pages are linked below rather than every individual post.
